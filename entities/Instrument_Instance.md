# Instrument Instance

## Definition
A particular device or tool used for scientific purposes, including the study of both natural phenomena and theoretical research.<sup>[1](#fn1)</sup>

## Usage notes
This entity represents an individual device.

Scientific instruments can vary in size and complexity. They arise from:
* serial manufacturing by a manufacturer 
* own development or adaptation at a research facility, possibly in cooperation with other organisations 
* bespoke development or adaptation by an external organisation
